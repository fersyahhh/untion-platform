# Untion (Understand Your Presentation)

Untion is an AI-powered web application designed to help users understand, study, and practice their PowerPoint (PPT) presentations. By leveraging third-party Large Language Models (LLMs), Untion acts as your personal presentation coach, providing comprehensive feedback and constructive corrections to elevate your public speaking skills and presentation quality.

## Core Features

- **AI-Powered Presentation Review**: Upload your PPT and practice your presentation. The AI will analyze the flow and context of your material.
- **Speech-to-Text Analysis**: Present your material live. Untion transcribes your speech and evaluates your delivery, pointing out mistakes and areas for improvement in your verbal presentation.
- **Content & Material Correction**: The AI scans your uploaded PPT to find typos, factual errors, or missing information, suggesting improvements to make your slides more effective.
- **Comprehensive Feedback**: Get a final review flow that holistically evaluates your understanding of the material, your speech delivery, and your visual aids.

## Upcoming Features

- **Multiplayer (Group Presentation) Mode**: Presentations are often collaborative. This upcoming feature will allow multiple users to practice together, ensuring that every group member understands the material and can present their specific parts cohesively.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4
- **Routing**: React Router v7
- **State Management**: Zustand
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Language**: TypeScript
